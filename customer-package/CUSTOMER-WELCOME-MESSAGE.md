### Step 1: Open Your Workspace
Access your application via the link provided on your Payhip download screen or open `index.html` from your downloaded folder in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).

We recommend bookmarking the page in your browser bar for quick daily access!

### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile (2 Minutes)
1. Click **"Edit Profile"** in the top navigation.
2. Select your business type (Coffee Shop, Restaurant, Salon, Barber, Gym, Dentist, Real Estate, or Local Services).
3. Type in your business name, city, and a few core offerings.
4. Hit **"Save Profile"**.

Notice how every single generator, weekly calendar topic, and review response template instantly updates to reflect your business!

### 💡 One Quick Golden Rule About Your Data
LocalBiz Kit uses a **Local-First Architecture**. All your business profiles, scheduled posts, and custom responses are saved directly inside your browser storage for complete privacy and offline speed.

**Pro-Tip**: Head to **Settings** and click **"Export JSON Backup"** once a week or whenever you draft important campaigns. That way, you'll always have a safe copy of your workspace on your computer!
