### Tired of guessing what to post on social media and struggling with Google reviews?

As a local business owner, your day is already packed with managing staff, greeting customers, handling inventory, and keeping operations running smoothly. The last thing you need at 9:00 PM is staring at a blank screen wondering what to post on Instagram or how to respond to an awkward customer review.

**LocalBiz Kit** is different. It is a clean, focused, browser-based marketing workspace designed specifically for brick-and-mortar shops and independent local services.

### Important Data Storage Information & Limitations

- **Local-First Privacy Architecture**: Your business details, drafts, and customer responses are stored securely inside your browser's private local cache (`localStorage` and `IndexedDB`). Your data is **not** transmitted to an external central server, ensuring complete confidentiality.
- **Manual Backups**: Because data lives on your device, we recommend using the built-in **"Export JSON Backup"** button in Settings periodically. If you manually clear your browser cookies and website data, you can easily restore your workspace using your backup file.
- **Manual Publishing**: To keep the app 100% subscription-free, LocalBiz Kit provides 1-click copy buttons rather than expensive, error-prone third-party API auto-posting. You retain complete editorial control before pasting into Instagram, Facebook, or Google.
