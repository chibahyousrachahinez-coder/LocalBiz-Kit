- LocalBiz Kit features a live character counter right inside the profile editor.
- The built-in generator drafts a structured description:
  - Line 1: Clear statement of what you do and where you are located.
  - Line 2: What makes your quality or experience stand out.
  - Line 3: Direct call-to-action (hours, parking, phone, or website).
- Click **"Copy Description"** and paste it directly into your Google Business Profile manager.

## 16. Important Data Storage & Privacy Information

Understanding how LocalBiz Kit stores your data is essential:

### 1. Browser-Local Storage (LocalStorage & IndexedDB)
- LocalBiz Kit operates under a **Local-First Architecture**.
- All your business information, saved drafts, and calendar entries are saved directly in your web browser's secure local database (`IndexedDB` and `localStorage`).
- **Your data is not uploaded to a remote central server.** This guarantees complete privacy for your business strategies, promotional margins, and customer message drafts.

### 2. Works 100% Offline
- Once the application is loaded in your browser, you can write copy, plan your calendar, and manage offers even without an active internet connection.

### 3. Crucial Limitation: Clearing Browser Data
- If you manually click **"Clear History and Website Data"** or use "Incognito/Private Browsing" mode, your browser will delete the stored website data.
- **Rule of Thumb**: Always click **"Export JSON Backup"** in Settings before performing deep browser maintenance!

**Q: Can I use LocalBiz Kit on multiple devices?**

## 19. Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: "I refreshed the page and my changes didn't appear."
- **Check**: Ensure you clicked the **"Save"** button inside the respective modal or profile editor before navigating away.
- **Check**: Verify that your browser is not in strict Incognito/Private mode, which prevents websites from saving persistent data.

### Issue: "The character count for my Google description says 780 characters."
- **Solution**: Google hard-caps descriptions at 750 characters. Trim unnecessary adjectives or simplify your call to action until the live character counter in the Business Profile view turns green.

### Issue: "I cleared my browser cache and my content disappeared."
- **Solution**: Go to **Settings > Restore / Import** and select your last exported JSON backup file (`localbizkit-[name]-backup.json`) to instantly recover your data.
