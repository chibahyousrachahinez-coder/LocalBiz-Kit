# LOCALBIZ KIT — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about using, maintaining, and getting the most value from LocalBiz Kit.

### 1. What exactly is LocalBiz Kit?
LocalBiz Kit is a clean, focused, browser-based marketing workspace designed for local, independent small business owners. It provides field-tested copy generators, an industry-tailored weekly content calendar, Google Business profile tools, review and DM reply templates, promotional offer creators, website copy generators, and a 21-item local presence audit.

### 3. Where is my business data stored?
LocalBiz Kit uses a **Local-First Architecture**. All your business profiles, generated posts, custom responses, scheduled calendar items, and checklist statuses are stored directly inside your own web browser's storage engine (`localStorage` and `IndexedDB`). 

Your private business data is **never uploaded to an external server or third-party cloud database**.

---

### 4. Will I lose my data if I refresh or close my browser?
**No.** Your data persists automatically in your browser's local cache. You can close your tabs, restart your browser, or reboot your computer, and your workspace will reload exactly where you left off.

---

### 5. What happens if I manually clear my browser cookies or history?
Web browsers treat local application storage as part of website data. If you click **"Clear All Website Data / Clear Cookies & Cache"** in your browser settings, your browser may delete locally stored app data.

**How to protect yourself**:
Go to **Settings** in LocalBiz Kit and click **"Export JSON Backup"** regularly (e.g., once a week or whenever you write new campaigns). That `.json` file saves your entire workspace to your computer.

---

### 6. How do I backup my workspace?
In the left sidebar, click **Settings**. You have two export options:
1. **Export JSON Backup (`.json`)**: A full machine-readable backup containing all your profile data, calendar schedules, custom offers, and responses. Keep this file in your Downloads, Google Drive, or Dropbox.
2. **Export Text Document (`.txt`)**: A clean, formatted summary of all your marketing copy and active offers that you can read, print, or email to a team member.

---

### 7. How do I transfer my data to a new computer?
1. On your current computer, open LocalBiz Kit, go to **Settings**, and click **Export JSON Backup**.
2. Transfer that downloaded `.json` file to your new computer (via email, USB, or cloud drive).
3. On your new computer, open LocalBiz Kit, navigate to **Settings > Restore / Import**, and upload the `.json` file. Your entire workspace will instantly populate.

---

### 8. Does LocalBiz Kit automatically post directly to Instagram, Facebook, or Google?
**No.** LocalBiz Kit is intentionally designed with 1-click clipboard copy buttons for manual pasting into your accounts.

---

### 9. Why does LocalBiz Kit use 1-click copy instead of API auto-posting?
We made this deliberate engineering choice for four practical reasons:
1. **Zero Recurring SaaS Subscriptions**: Direct social media APIs charge developer platforms thousands of dollars every month, which forces software to charge you $29–$99/month. By keeping publishing client-side, we can offer LocalBiz Kit as an affordable one-time purchase.
2. **No Constant Token Disconnections**: Social media platforms frequently expire connection tokens, causing scheduled posts to silently fail without the business owner knowing.
3. **Total Editorial Control**: Local business marketing requires nuance. A quick 1-click copy allows you to review the text on your phone or laptop, add a fresh photo of your storefront, and post with complete confidence.
4. **Platform Formats**: You can paste into Instagram, Facebook, Google Updates, WhatsApp Business, Nextdoor, or email newsletters without platform restrictions.

### 15. What is the Google Business Profile 750-character limit?
Google limits the public business description on Google Business Profiles (Google Maps) to exactly **750 characters**. If you exceed this, Google truncates your copy or rejects the update. LocalBiz Kit features a real-time character counter and optimizer to ensure your description fits within the limit while highlighting your location, USPs, and contact details.
