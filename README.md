## 🌟 Overview

Most marketing software is built for corporate enterprise teams with complex dashboards, bloated analytics, and $50–$150/month recurring subscriptions.

**LocalBiz Kit** is built specifically for local business owners, operators, and managers who need high-converting, professional marketing output in minutes without the subscription trap or marketing jargon.

It runs directly in modern browsers, works completely offline, keeps 100% of your business data and customer reply drafts private on your own device, and generates field-tested copy, weekly calendars, promotional kits, review responses, and website sections in seconds.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🏢 Business Profile & 750-Character Google Optimizer
- Configure your business name, trade category, location, core offerings, and brand tone (*Friendly, Professional, Playful, Premium, Local/community*).
- All generators across the app dynamically adapt to your profile.
- Built-in **750-Character Google Business Description Optimizer** with a live character counter to ensure your Google Maps listing is compelling and never truncated.

### 2. ✍️ Content Studio (Create, Edit & Schedule)
- Generate social captions, hooks, and updates for **Instagram**, **Facebook**, and **Google Updates**.
- Choose from trade-specific topics or enter custom prompts.
- Refine output inline with full text editing.
- Schedule drafts directly into the weekly calendar with 1 click.
- 1-click clipboard copying formatted with appropriate hashtags and spacing.

### 3. 📅 7-Day Weekly Content Calendar
- Built around a proven weekly posting rhythm:
  - **Monday**: Education & Behind-the-Scenes
  - **Tuesday**: Product / Service Spotlight
  - **Wednesday**: Midweek Problem Solver
  - **Thursday**: Customer Stories & Social Proof
  - **Friday**: Weekend Promotions & Specials
  - **Saturday**: Community Vibes & Neighborhood Life
  - **Sunday**: Reset & Operating Hours
- Filter and toggle statuses: `Draft`, `Scheduled`, and `Published`.

### 4. 📍 Google Business Toolkit & Q&A Prep
- 4 dedicated Google Post creators: **What's New**, **Special Offer**, **Product Spotlight**, and **Event Notice**.
- 5-part customer Q&A preparation module covering parking, reservations, custom orders, accessibility, and payment methods.

### 5. 💬 Customer Responses Library (Reviews, DMs & Emails)
- **Review Replies**: Pre-drafted, de-escalating, and appreciative responses for 5-star, 4-star, 3-star, and critical 1–2 star reviews.
- **Direct Messages**: Rapid responses for pricing, availability, hours, location, and custom requests.
- **Client Emails**: Professional quote follow-ups, appointment confirmations, and win-back emails.
- Dynamic placeholders automatically populate your business name, city, phone, and email.

### 6. 🏷️ Offer Builder & Countertop Sign Generator
- 6 structured local promotion formulas:
  1. First-Time Visitor Welcome Pass
  2. Limited-Time Seasonal Special
  3. Neighborhood VIP / Regulars Perk
  4. Midweek Slow-Day Booster
  5. Bundle & Save Deal
  6. Referral / Friend Incentive
- Automatically produces campaign headlines, clean terms & conditions, ready-to-print countertop sign text, and social announcements.

### 7. 🌐 5-Section Website Copy Generator
- Drafts complete, conversion-focused landing page copy:
  - **Hero Section**: Catchy headline, value proposition, and call-to-action buttons.
  - **Services Grid**: 3 core offerings with customer benefits.
  - **Why Choose Us**: 3 local trust pillars.
  - **Local FAQ Accordion**: 4 realistic neighborhood questions and answers.
  - **Visit & Contact Block**: Hours, location, parking advice, and contact details.
- Export directly to Markdown (`.md`) or plain text (`.txt`).

### 8. ✅ 21-Item Local Marketing Checklist & Presence Score
- Interactive audit broken into 4 categories:
  - Google Business Profile (6 items)
  - Website Essentials (5 items)
  - Social Media Presence (5 items)
  - Customer Feedback & Retention (5 items)
- Real-time **Online Presence Score** (0% to 100%) tracking your digital footprint.

### 9. 📚 32-Item Template & Hook Library
- Browse categorized hooks, captions, and customer engagement formulas.
- Instant search and 1-click favorite bookmarking.

### 10. 💾 Data Ownership & Backup Portability
- **JSON Backup Export**: Download your complete workspace state in one `.json` file.
- **Text Document Export**: Generate a formatted `.txt` report containing all active copy, offers, and schedules.
- **Restore & Reset**: Import past JSON backups or reset to the pre-loaded "Harbor & Bean" demo store anytime.

## 🔒 Data Privacy & Storage Model

LocalBiz Kit is built with a **Local-First Architecture**:
- **Browser-Local Storage**: All business profiles, calendar entries, custom responses, and checklist progress are stored in your browser's private `localStorage` and `IndexedDB`.
- **Zero Cloud Tracking**: No private customer messages, pricing strategies, or business plans are transmitted to a central backend database.
- **100% Offline Capable**: Once loaded, you can plan campaigns, draft posts, and write replies without an active internet connection.
- **Safe Backups**: Use the built-in **Settings > Export JSON Backup** feature to store safe offline copies on your computer or cloud drive.

## 💻 Quick Start & Development

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (version 18.0.0 or higher recommended)
- `npm` or `bun`

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/localbiz-kit.git
   cd localbiz-kit
   ```

2. **Install dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the development server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open `http://localhost:3000` in your web browser.

4. **Build for production**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
   The production-ready static assets will be output to the `dist/` directory.

5. **Run type-checking & linter**:
   ```bash
   npm run lint
   ```
